# Research and writing evaluation — 2026-09-06

The rebuild was benchmarked **before new bulk collection**, then revised against demonstrated failures. It improved several ideation and scope-handling cases. More research did not automatically improve short ad bodies: the recovered stack repeatedly matched or beat expanded rewrites. That finding led to a separate, concise body-only path rather than forcing every rewrite through full-script development.

## Method

Twenty real historical cases were recovered privately: sixteen rejected drafts, one approved correction and three positive examples. Four client ad-body tasks retained exact original requests. Only ten cases had enough original context to support controlled comparisons; the other ten remained diagnostics because their requests were missing or reconstructed. Hidden feedback was excluded from writer inputs.

Writers and independent judges used `gpt-6-astra` with high reasoning through an isolated Codex CLI. The recovered and rebuilt instruction stacks received the same frozen case inputs. Arm labels were randomized separately by case, and judges received neither the arm key nor a preferred outcome. Prompts, outputs, traces, schemas, source hashes, eligibility and keys were saved privately. This tests instruction-stack behavior on one model; it does not isolate an upgrade from an older model.

Six equally weighted editorial dimensions scored task/format fit, fact fidelity, audience relevance, useful progression, opening/payoff/product fit and natural usable delivery. Scores are descriptive model judgments, not validated measures of creative quality. Pairwise preferences, including ties and insufficient briefs, remain authoritative for the reported comparison.

Clean-v1 recorded two rebuilt preferences and eight insufficient briefs. Clean-v2 recorded two rebuilt preferences, one tie and seven insufficient briefs. Missing requests limit what can be concluded even when a draft looks better.

## Short ad-body failure and response

Across the four exact client body tasks, Clean-v0 had one recovered preference and three ties; Clean-v1 had two recovered preferences and two ties; Clean-v2 had three recovered preferences and one tie. The broad writing path added repeated explanations, sometimes weakened cadence, and did not establish a meaningful overall improvement for this subset.

The installed skill therefore has a dedicated [body-rewrite procedure](../skills/viral-script/references/body-rewrite.md). It preserves a coherent existing argument, adds only a missing logical connection, checks actual sentence redundancy and keeps offer qualifications in scope. A targeted replay of all four exact body tasks produced **four ties against the recovered stack**, with no rebuilt wins or losses. It recovered parity on this small exposed set; it did not establish superior paid copy. This was a developmental retest after reviewing failures, not independent validation.

## Fresh creative-format check

Eight [fictional briefs](fresh-briefs.json) and [criteria](fresh-criteria.md) tested paid UGC, claymation, skit, jumbotron, organic education, aware paid creative, body-only scope and ideation after a topic rejection. The first independent review rated six usable candidates and requested revisions to the skit and jumbotron. A subsequent replay rated seven usable and still requested a skit revision.

The jumbotron's initial reveal demanded too many sequential actions in five seconds. The skit initially made a character choose an already assigned slot just to demonstrate blocking; its replay still resolved the conflict before the product mattered. The delivered [sample set](../examples/README.md) includes an editor-curated correction: the character secures another available slot before conceding the room. Other curated edits remove a repeated bridge, reduce reveal text and correct an overstatement. Curated samples are separate from the frozen writer outputs and have not been regraded, owner-approved, filmed or performance-tested.

Most fresh outputs were clear and usable but conventional. Better instructions are not a substitute for a distinctive audience insight, revealing footage, a real offer or an excellent performance. The next useful evaluation is an unseen real brief with attributable owner feedback, followed by actual audience and business outcomes.
