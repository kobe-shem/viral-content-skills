# Actual paid creative: five advertiser libraries

Collected and inspected on 2026-09-06. These are actual ad-library creatives, selected for distinct arguments and formats. They are **not verified profitable ads**. Public delivery dates do not reveal spend, targeting, conversions, or the funnel job. Brand attribution does not mean the named creator appears in every ad.

Coverage: full files downloaded and hashed; opening frames at 0.1, 0.5, 1, 2 and 3 seconds plus seven distributed frames per creative inspected; provider transcripts read, with separate local word-timed ASR used to check missing speech. Time anchors below are approximate transcript/sample positions. This supports script and sampled visual observations. Continuous playback, musical feel, performance nuance, and exact caption/transition timing remain unreviewed. Do not turn these notes into a calibrated editing preset.

## What changes in new creative

Start with the job and argument, then choose the opening's information channels. Use persistent labels for comparisons, diagrams for relationships, proof inserts for relevant objections, physical staging for a legible discrepancy, and uninterrupted action for a demonstration's decisive step. Keep these as options with conditions. The sources do not establish a universal caption color, cut rate, speaking pace, or profitable script formula.
