# Sources and evidence scope

Use primary sources to understand what a creator teaches, then compare their actual strong, ordinary, and weak creative. The skill's recommendations are a synthesis, not a claim that any creator endorses this package. Source performance claims remain attributed to that source.

Start with [creator decisions](creator-decisions.md) for the five priority creators, new primary lessons and inspected visual examples. This file retains the more granular recovered Nathan citations and source-scope rules.

## Other source families

- **Alex Hormozi:** the official [Mozi Media Content Method Pt I](https://www.acquisition.com/training/leads5) supplies the previously missing hook/retain/reward lesson. Its full media and ASR were recovered; precise windows appear in the creator card. Privately available Hooks and GOATed Ads playbooks support the callout/value test and structured variation. Complete books are not distributed here.
- **Ava Yuergens / Personal Brand Launch:** three official lessons now supply primary hook/scripting/storytelling teaching, and inspected original media supplies scoped visual examples. The older caption-only study still cannot establish on-screen words or editing; its caption proxy, demographic transfer assumptions and inferred DM automation are excluded.
- **Adley Kinsman:** verified original channel media and her interview supply visual-question/payoff examples and attributed teaching. Original entertainment media is distinct from an advertiser's measured product demonstration.
- **Daniel Iles / Viral Coach:** full free-training transcript and original short-form media supply delivery, format and funnel-stage evidence. Public ad-library dates remain presence evidence; longevity does not prove profitability. His attention-first paid claim requires downstream outcome measurement.
- **Ronny Mitchell:** source-attributed course/video material supports audience demand, useful supply, differentiation, a coherent narrative line, defined terms, actionable explanations, and polishing spoken logic. When a guest supplies a teaching, attribute the speaker correctly.
- **Devin Jatho:** organic craft research supports studying idea/hook/delivery together and comparing coached cases with typical content. Its observational sample does not prove the coaching caused the outcomes or establish a paid-ad formula.

## Rule evidence

For project-specific research, store the original URL/ID, source date, capture date, timecoded evidence, collection method, modality coverage, and confidence alongside the rule. The [research method](../../viral-research/references/research-method.md) defines the evidence classes and coverage requirements.

Do not upgrade a teaching into a law because several summaries repeat it. Do not treat an old report's word “verified” as a substitute for checking what was actually verified: arithmetic, identity, visible behavior, and campaign performance are different questions.
