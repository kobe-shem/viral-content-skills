# Viral research

For original media preparation and multimodal observation, use [audiovisual study](references/audiovisual.md) and the bundled `scripts/media.py`. Prepared frames and extracted audio still require review.

Produce evidence that changes a creative decision. Begin with the requested brand, audience, platform, objective, format, and question. Read [research-method.md](references/research-method.md); read the applicable provider instructions only when using that provider.

Use existing project research, source media, authorized account exports, and known course/SOP material first. A new scrape is justified by stale data, missing platforms/formats, missing comparisons, or missing modalities. A cached transcript may be reusable even when views need refreshing.

## Provider selection

- **Sandcastles:** discover channels, topic/format patterns, and account-relative outliers; retrieve selected video details/transcripts/analyses.
- **Apify:** acquire account/post histories, metadata, and comments; use a verified actor's current schema and preserve raw output.
- **Foreplay:** find advertiser identities, paid creatives, transcripts, landing destinations, variants, and longevity. Own-account performance, when authorized and available, is separate from public competitor data.
- **Primary sources:** official creator channels, original videos, and course/SOP material the user can access. Distinguish the creator's teaching from observed behavior and third-party summaries.

See [providers.md](references/providers.md) for current invocation procedures and the portable helper. Discover available MCP tools rather than inventing names or assuming a prior machine's connection exists. Use the configured account and task budget; never copy another machine's credentials into the skill.

## Corpus and comparisons

Resolve creator/advertiser identity using source-backed handles and URLs. Different people with similar names must remain separate. Record retrieval dates, exact query/account IDs, pagination/completeness, deleted/private/unavailable items, and provider limits. “No result” means no result under that search—not that a creator has no ads or no channel.

Collect the available catalog/history appropriate to the request, then select distinct high, typical, and weak examples within comparable platform/format/time cohorts. Preserve selection criteria, baseline definition, sample size, post age, duplicates/reposts, and unknown promotion status. Do not label public low-view posts unprofitable or high-view posts commercial winners.

Where practical, hide performance labels during craft annotation, then join the metrics afterward. Use provider outlier scores only with their stated definition and limits. A tiny baseline can create a spectacular multiple.

## Complete creative inspection

For selected examples, examine the full script/story progression, first useful reward, payoff, product introduction, objections/proof, and CTA. Inspect first-frame composition, physical action/props, camera, exact screen text, spoken/text/visual alignment, blocking, cuts, graphic timing, captions, B-roll, sound, and performance.

Preserve modality coverage. A caption is not the spoken hook; a transcript is not the picture; contact sheets are not full audiovisual playback. Measurement tools propose events but do not establish their meaning. Label secondary model/provider analysis separately from direct observation. Keep unknowns visible rather than manufacturing a complete teardown.

Use `video-style-study` when available for measured editing studies. Otherwise retain timecoded observations in the portable evidence format. Keep source footage and complete course transcripts in the user's project; the reusable library contains concise paraphrases, original source pointers, conditions, and counterexamples.

## Synthesis

Give each candidate rule a condition, action, intended purpose, source/timecodes, evidence class, exceptions, and confidence. Compare what a creator teaches with their strong and weak examples. Do not let a source's sales claims become independently verified performance or the target brand's claims.

Return the coverage ledger, useful findings, reference examples, unresolved gaps, and concrete implications for ideas/scripts/direction. Route the next requested stage through `viral-ideate`, `viral-script`, or `viral-direct`. A giant scrape without a usable creative decision is unfinished research.
