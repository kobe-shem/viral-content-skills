# Paid claymation: ShelfCheck

Fictional brief and product. Fresh-v2 candidate; independent review marked usable, with production/taste caveats. No owner approval, recorded timing or performance result.

Approximately 30 seconds; timing estimated. Vertical tabletop claymation. All counting, thresholds and screens are illustrative.

EXACT VO
“An empty shelf tells you what’s already gone. To spot a low count earlier, someone has to count what’s there. In ShelfCheck, staff record the count for an item. Each item has a reorder threshold. Below it? The app flags the count. A person still decides what to order and places that order. Watch the count-to-alert demo.”

B1 — 0–5s | Discover the gap
Scene: A clay shopkeeper holds a tray beside an empty shelf bay. Their free hand reaches into the bay, then stops.
Camera: Fixed close three-quarter view; empty bay fills the center.
Text: “Already empty.” Above the shelf. Persistent readable corner label throughout: “Illustrative animation.”
Sound: One soft tray contact; no alarm.
VO: “An empty shelf tells you what’s already gone.”

B2 — 5–10s | Establish the human input
Cut to a separate worked example: three clay jars on a shelf labeled “JAM.” Shopkeeper points to each jar once.
Camera: Straight-on close view, all three jars visible throughout.
Text: “Example: 3 jars.”
Sound: Three light counting taps.
VO: “To spot a low count earlier, someone has to count what’s there.”

B3 — 10–17s | Record and compare
Shopkeeper presses a large clay keypad tile marked “3.” An illustrative screen now reads “ShelfCheck / JAM / Count: 3 / Reorder threshold: 5.” Keep that screen flat to camera. The threshold is already present; do not animate it being inferred from the count.
Text: Screen contents only, plus the persistent illustration label.
Sound: One soft entry tap.
VO: “In ShelfCheck, staff record the count for an item. Each item has a reorder threshold.”

B4 — 17–21s | Show the app’s action
Hold the same screen. An amber flag unfolds beside Count: 3. Add “Below threshold.” Keep both 3 and 5 visible.
Camera: Small push toward the comparison; no additional transformation.
Sound: One restrained alert tone.
VO: “Below it? The app flags the count.”

B5 — 21–27s | Preserve the person’s decision
Cut wider. Shopkeeper looks from the flagged screen to an ordinary paper order form, writes “Jam: 4,” then deliberately stamps “ORDER PLACED.” The paper is physically separate from the app screen. No stock appears or moves automatically.
Text: “Person chooses + places order.”
Sound: Pencil stroke, then stamp.
VO: “A person still decides what to order and places that order.”

B6 — 27–30s | CTA
Hold the flagged screen beside the completed paper form.
Text: “ShelfCheck / Watch the count-to-alert demo.”
VO: “Watch the count-to-alert demo.”

Assets: one clay shopkeeper, shelf, tray, three jars, illustrative screen/keypad, paper form, pencil and stamp. Build text as crisp graphic overlays. Keep jar count and item identity consistent. Captions follow the VO separately from screen labels.
