# Organic education: a usable handoff

Fictional brief and product. Astra editor-curated candidate from fresh-v2; not independently regraded after these edits. No owner approval, recorded timing or performance result.

Approximately 35 seconds; timing estimated. Vertical desk demonstration for junior project managers.

EXACT SPOKEN SCRIPT
“Could someone act on this without messaging you? Give them four things: an owner, a next action, a due date, and evidence that it’s done.

‘Review the landing page’ leaves too much open.

Try: ‘Maya, review the draft landing page for unclear copy by Thursday at three. Leave comments in the draft, then reply “review complete” with the link.’

Now Maya knows what to do, when it’s due, and what to send back. That’s a handoff someone can use.”

OPENING — 0–4s
Locked overhead camera. A card reading “Review the landing page” sits alone on a desk. Presenter slides it toward an empty receiving position, then stops with one finger on the card while asking the opening question.
Exact screen hook: “Would this need a follow-up message?” Across the upper portion, clear of the card.
Persistent small label during the example: “Fictional example.”

EARLY VALUE — 4–11s
On “four things,” place one prepared strip beneath the card: “OWNER | NEXT ACTION | DUE DATE | COMPLETION EVIDENCE.” Reveal the whole strip immediately and hold while naming the four fields.

WORKED EXAMPLE — 11–27s
On “‘Review the landing page,’” point once to the original card. On “Try,” replace it with a larger prepared handoff card. Reveal these four lines in sync with speech:
“OWNER: Maya”
“NEXT ACTION: Review draft landing page for unclear copy”
“DUE: Thursday, 3 p.m.”
“DONE: Comments in draft + ‘review complete’ reply with link”
Use a close crop and generous line spacing. Keep each revealed line visible as the next appears.

PAYOFF — 27–35s
Slide the completed card into the same receiving position where the original stopped. Hold it there through the final sentence. Exact closing text: “Owner. Action. Due. Evidence.”

Keep the paper movement and speech audible without music. If captions are used, place them below the card; do not cover its fields. End on the completed example with no commercial CTA.
