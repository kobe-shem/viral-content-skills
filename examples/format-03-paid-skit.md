# Paid skit: RoomClock

Fictional brief and product. Astra editor-curated candidate from fresh-v2; not independently regraded after these edits. No owner approval, recorded timing or performance result.

Approximately 30 seconds; timing estimated. Two coworkers, one meeting room.

SETUP
Alex wants the room for a client call and stands outside the doorway, screen left, holding a paper marked “CLIENT CALL — 2:00.” Sam wants a quiet place to record narration and sits inside, screen right, behind the laptop. Sam’s paper sign on the door reads “RECORDING — 2:00.” These are separate plans, not existing RoomClock reservations.

0–5s
Picture: Medium doorway two-shot. Alex’s hand is already on the door; Alex pushes it open as Sam raises one finger for silence.
Alex: “My client call’s in here.”
Sam, whispering: “I’m recording.”
Exact opening text, upper center: “One room. Two plans.”
Sound: Door movement, then room tone.

5–11s
Alex lifts their paper: “I put two o’clock in the chat.”
Sam points toward the door sign: “I put it on the door.”
Cut to Alex looking from the sign to their own paper. Hold the realization for a beat.

11–16s
Alex: “They’re joining now.”
Sam exhales, drops the whisper: “Find me another slot first.”
Sam turns the laptop toward Alex. Alex enters the room; maintain Alex on the left and Sam on the right.

16–23s
Over-shoulder laptop insert. Show a clearly labeled illustrative RoomClock calendar with the room’s 2:00 and 3:00 slots available. Sam points to 3:00 and books it. That slot changes to “Booked” and its booking control becomes unavailable; 2:00 remains available.
Sam: “Three’s open. I’ll take that.”
Alex selects and books the remaining 2:00 slot: “Two’s mine.”
Screen heading: “RoomClock.” Small readable label: “Illustrative demo.” Do not imitate an unprovided actual interface or show calendar integrations.

23–27s
Return to the doorway framing. Sam takes down the recording sign and steps out. Alex stays with the laptop.
Alex: “Thanks.”
Sam, quietly but firmly: “Leave me the room. At three.”
Alex nods.

27–30s
Hold on the laptop’s two separate booked slots.
VO: “RoomClock. See the shared calendar.”
Exact end text: “RoomClock / See the shared calendar.”

Captions reproduce dialogue. Cut on changes in who controls the room and on the booking action. No music is necessary; leave room for the pause after the competing paper plans.
