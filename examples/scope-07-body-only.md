# Paid ad body only: RoomClock

Fictional brief and product. Astra editor-curated candidate from fresh-v2; not independently regraded after these edits. No owner approval, recorded timing or performance result.

When room bookings live in separate messages, each person can think they’ve arranged it. They only discover the clash at the door, with a meeting about to start. Now someone has to find another space or move their meeting. RoomClock shows what’s available and blocks a slot once it’s booked, so the next person can choose another time.
