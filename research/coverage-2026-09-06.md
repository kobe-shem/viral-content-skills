September 6 research pass. It separates provider rows, normalized creator-owned records, selected
media inspections, teaching transcripts, and model interpretation. Counts describe captured
evidence. They do not establish full account history, organic distribution, creative causality, or
commercial performance.

Public teaching and channel sources are listed in [`source-index.json`](./source-index.json). Raw
provider payloads, full media, transcripts, frames, and account records remain outside the portable
repository.

## Paid-ad evidence

The paid-ad provider returned **1,004 records** for the five target advertisers. Personal Brand
Launch retained a separate exact-name alternate identity that returned zero rows rather than being
silently merged with the verified page:

- **724** have provider timestamped transcripts.
- Four large advertiser pulls stopped at 250 rows with a cursor remaining.
- Personal Brand Launch exposed four current retrievable videos on its verified page. That result is
  distinct from the provider's larger historical discovery count and is not a claim of four lifetime
  ads.

Fifteen distinct paid video concepts, three per creator, were preserved as full local files. All 15
have local ASR, deterministic media metadata, scene-cut candidates, loudness/silence measurements,
and 12 sampled frames each (**180 frames total**). The sampled frames were reviewed for visible
structure. The machine measurements are complete in the private study record.

The paid panel does not contain spend, impressions, reach, hold rate, clicks, conversions, revenue,
or profit. Ad age, repeated capture, or continued availability is a retrieval signal rather than a
performance result. Scene thresholds can misread overlays, motion, and dissolves as cuts. Loudness
and silence measurements do not identify music, masking, sound-design intent, or subjective feel.
The report therefore makes no full-playback, full-audio-listening, or profitability claim.

## Secondary scene analysis

A secondary video-analysis model completed one public YouTube source per creator: **five videos and
116 labeled scenes** in total. Each result supplies time-ranged visual, shot-type, label, and audio
descriptions. Captions and sampled frames were available for reconciliation.

These records are provider interpretation. They have not been calibrated against exhaustive human
scene annotation, uninterrupted listening, or a known audiovisual ground truth. They can suggest
where to inspect; they cannot establish an exact shot list, verbatim transcript, music taxonomy,
speaker identity, or causal editing effect. The five results remain separate from primary teaching
and observed-performance evidence.

The OAuth video-research MCP was not live-authenticated during this snapshot. Historical detail
responses could contain angle, topic, spoken-hook, format, visual-layout, and narrative labels, but
did not provide a full transcript, timestamped transcript, audio analysis, shot list, or complete
scene inspection. No claims in this report depend on a live call to that route.
